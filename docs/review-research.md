# Balibu review research

Checked **2 October 2026** to inform original website wording, not to publish testimonials or a live review feed.

## Review evidence

Read **three comments** on [Restaurant Guru’s Balibu listing](https://restaurantguru.com/Balibu-Invercargill), each labelled as originating on Google. Its address, phone and website match Balibu at 39 Esk Street, Invercargill.

| Date displayed | Evidence read, paraphrased |
| --- | --- |
| 3 months ago | Praise for food generally, a milkshake and service. |
| 4 months ago | Praise for vanilla ice cream and a staff member accommodating a request near closing. |
| A year ago | Praise for a slushie and service. |

All three mention positive service experiences; sweets and drinks are the specific products discussed. No named savoury dish appears in this sample.

These were the landing page’s default selection, with no visible sorting or filtering control. They are not a representative survey. No mixed or negative comment appeared in these three; the [full reviews page](https://restaurantguru.com/Balibu-Invercargill/reviews?bylang=1) failed to load, so this does not establish an absence of criticism. Relative dates are recorded as displayed, not converted into exact dates.

The [verified Google Maps listing](https://www.google.com/maps/search/?api=1&query=Balibu%2C%2039%20Esk%20Street%2C%20Invercargill&query_place_id=ChIJc9Ge1QjF0qkR_iGknNBeoKc) did not expose review text in the anonymous browser view; Google Search presented a CAPTCHA. This research therefore used Google-origin comments displayed by Restaurant Guru, not a direct Google review corpus.

## Menu facts and copy guidance

[Balibu’s public merchant menu on Uber Eats](https://www.ubereats.com/nz/store/balibu/ruAgSzoUUAG1f7EOj4s-BQ) separately lists Indonesian meals, noodles, chicken rendang with rice, bakso, shakes, smoothies, slushies and ice cream. These are menu facts, not review findings; availability and pricing should come from the current ordering menu.

Use a welcoming voice and give drinks and dessert some attention alongside the savoury food. An original direction is: **Indonesian comfort food. A warm welcome. Something sweet to finish.**

Do not turn this limited sample into broad consensus, rankings, awards, or claims that a particular savoury dish is a customer favourite. Do not reuse reviewer wording as marketing copy or imply that the proposed wording is a testimonial.
