# Higgsfield dish plates

Generated 7 October 2026 with the Higgsfield CLI on the free plan, model **Seedream 5.0 Flash** (`seedream_5_0_flash`), 1:1, 2K (2048 × 2048), 0.5 credits each. No reference images were supplied. Each output was a plate on a plain white background; the background and drop shadow were removed locally (circle mask from the plate rim, plus saturated overhang such as satay skewers), cropped square with a 4% margin and resized to 1600 × 1600 RGBA PNG. Edges were checked on the site's red, green and cream fields. Each file carries its prompt in PNG metadata.

These are generated illustrations of dishes on Balibu's menu, not photographs of Balibu's food. Replace them with real photography when it is available.
