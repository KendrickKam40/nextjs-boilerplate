# Surface brief: public homepage (components/site)

Scope: the whole public site at `/`, one long-scroll page. Visitor mode: Persuade.
Audience: Invercargill locals and Esk Eats foot traffic on phones first; online orderers and newcomers next.
Action: Order online (provider handoff), or walk up to T.29. Proof: real dishes, live menu and prices when the POS is connected, real counter photo, published hours.
Constraints: admin controls section order and visibility and theme colours; deep links #menu #about #flavours #contact; no invented prices, ratings or testimonials; fonts self-hosted.
References pinned by the user: vibebevvy.com, palmo.co.in, drinkstill.nz ("drastic, WOW and impact").

## Direction contract

THESIS: Balibu as a gerobak: the hand-painted food cart and warung signboard, in loud painted colour fields and fat sign lettering. It refuses the cream-paper editorial page with italic serif and hairline rules that the old site and the category default share.

OWN-WORLD: Drenched fields of sambal red, pandan green and palm-sugar yellow, with coconut-cream type. Bungee block capitals for every headline, Yellowtail sign-writer's script for asides, Manrope for reading. A paint-shadow offset on display lettering only. Pill buttons like painted plates, a scrolling ticker band between fields, and real food cutouts at huge scale.

STORY: The visitor feels the noise and appetite of a food cart, sees exactly what is cooking (menu, prices when live, sweets), learns it's at T.29 Esk Eats and open now, and orders online or walks up.

FIRST VIEWPORT: A full-bleed sambal-red field. "BIG FLAVOUR." in Bungee spans the width in cream, with a palm-yellow paint shadow. "Bali soul." in Yellowtail cuts across it. The nasi goreng plate, about 45% of the width, overlaps the lettering and turns as you scroll. One line of copy sits bottom-left, with the cream "Order online" pill and the open chip. A sticky top bar holds the logo, section links and Order, and a ticker band starts at the fold.

FORM: Pinned by the user's references (no roll); the gerobak sign-painting world is my first-ranked grounded direction. No seed key.

FINISH: unreviewed and undocumented is unfinished; this build ends with the finish review, the verdict, DESIGN.md, and every shipping raster carrying its provenance
